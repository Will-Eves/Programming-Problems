Solution: Iteratively collapse the states of the problem

Not all ?,|,- need to be collapsed because a remaining ?,|,- at the end of the iteration
means that it could guarenteed have grown from some direction

Best way to implement is a boolean array of [could_be_right, could_be_bottom, could_be_root]
where . = [False, False, False] and ? = [True, True, True]

Base State:

? -> from right OR from bottom OR root
. -> empty

Mixed States:

| -> from bottom OR root
- -> from right OR root
x -> from right OR bottom

Low States:

> -> from right
v -> from bottom
r -> root
